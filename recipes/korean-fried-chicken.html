Korean Fried Chicken

Description

Delicious meal, can be very spicy!

Ingredients:

  • Chicken breast or whole chicken
  • Eggs
  • Gochujang (korean red paste)
  • Flour
  • Panko

Steps:

  1. Cover the chicken pieces in a mixture of flour and eggs
  2. Dip the chicken pieces in panko
  3. Fry the chicken in frying oil, until it gets a gold crisp look
  4. Boil gochujang (korean red paste) and dip the fried chicken pieces in it
