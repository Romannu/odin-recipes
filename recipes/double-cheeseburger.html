Double Cheeseburger

Description

Two 100% beef patties topped with two slices of melted cheddar cheese, onions, ketchup, mustard, and pickles.

Ingredients:

  • Two beef patties;
  • Ketchup;
  • Mustard;
  • Pickles;
  • Minced onions;
  • Cheddar cheese;
  • Two bread buns.

Steps:

  1. Grill the patties
  2. Roast a little bit the bread buns
  3. Add the mustard to the bread
  4. Add the ketchup to the bread
  5. Add the patties on top of the bun
  6. Add the cheese so it melts on top of the patties
  7. Add the pickles and onions on top of everything
  8. Close with the other bun
